Crossplay strategy

The center star tells you where the first word sits. A tile on the star scores its face value. The points on an opening are the double letters on row 8 and the double word the word can still reach. This board has 8 double-word squares and 20 triple-letter squares. Each corner is a triple letter. The triple word on that edge is three squares in from the corner.

K is 6, V is 6, and W is 5. G is 4 and U is 2. Those tiles earn their keep on a letter bonus. The sweep is 40, and it is added after every multiplier on the word. Four tiles on a corner can outscore seven tiles that never reach a word bonus. The bag holds three blanks. A blank scores 0, including on a triple letter, and it still carries a word bonus for the other letters. When one play offers both squares, the blank belongs on the triple word.

QUICKLY on an empty Crossplay board

Type QUICKLY on an empty board and run Show Me Best Move. The first lines are Across at 8,6 and Down at 6,8. Both score 128. Q is on the double letter in column 6, so the 10 becomes 20. K is on the double letter in column 10, so the 6 becomes 12. Y is on the double word in column 12. I is on the star.

JINX on the Crossplay corner

JINX across row 1, columns 1–4, puts J on the corner triple letter and X on the triple word. J is 10 × 3 = 30 before the word is tripled, so J's share of the total is 90. Four tiles leave the rack, so there is no sweep. Row 1 leaves the star empty. The play needs a tile already on the board to hook to. Any cross-word is added after the 120.

Frequently asked questions

How do I read a Crossplay board from a screenshot?+

Tap Upload Screenshot and choose a picture of the NYT Crossplay game, or paste it, or drop it on the grid. The board and the rack are read on your device. The picture stays on the device. Fix any letter that looks wrong in the review grid, then apply it.

What does the Crossplay center star score?+

The star marks row 8, column 8, and the first word has to cover it. A tile played there scores that letter's face value. In the QUICKLY opening, I is the star tile and it scores 1.

Why does QUICKLY score 128 on an empty Crossplay board?+

Show Me Best Move lists Across at 8,6 and Down at 6,8 at 128. Q on the double letter is 20, K on the double letter is 12, and the double word under Y doubles the 44-point letter total to 88. The sweep adds 40. The centered line, Across at 8,5, scores 72.

Where should a blank sit on JINX at the corner?+

JINX across row 1, columns 1 through 4, scores 120 with J on the triple letter and X on the triple word. The blank scores 0. With the blank as X, the main word is 96. With the blank as J, the main word is 30. The blank belongs on the triple word, where it still triples the other letters.

How many blanks can the Crossplay rack hold?+

The Crossplay bag has three blanks. This rack accepts three question marks and refuses a fourth. Type each blank as ?.

Which word list does this Crossplay solver use?+

NYT Crossplay plays NWL2023, 196,601 words, minus 182 trademark and proper-noun cuts, which leaves 196,419. This page scores 178,562 words: TWL06, plus DA, GI, PO, TE, EW, and OK, minus 135 words NASPA has dropped. The 107 two-letter words match. 17,991 longer Crossplay words are missing. With Crossplay selected, the solver also skips the Times cuts that are still in the file. The word checker separates a missing word from a Times cut.

What does Get a Hint reveal?+

Get a Hint starts with the top score. The next taps add the word length, Across or Down and the area of the board, how many rack tiles the play uses, and the first letter. Five taps is the last clue. Show Me Best Move is the full list.

Does the top Crossplay score include the bag?+

The figure is the score for this turn. Tiles still in the bag, and the tiles on the other rack, are outside it. QUICKLY at 128 spends Q and K. A smaller line that keeps one of those tiles can be the better leave.

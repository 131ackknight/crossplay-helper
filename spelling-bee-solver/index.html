          How to use the Spelling Bee solver

          1. Enter the letters. Type the center letter in the blue box, then the other six in any order. You can paste all seven at once, like MAFINRY.
          2. Get hints. Press Get hints. The Today's puzzle button loads the Times' letters and its official answer list.
          3. Read the grid. Each row of the hint grid is a first letter and each column is a word length. The two-letter starts narrow it down.
          4. Track your words. Type the words you have found. The grid counts down and your rank updates. Tap a hidden word only when you are stuck.

          How Spelling Bee scoring works

          Every word needs four or more letters and has to use the center letter. Letters can repeat, so MAMMA counts with one M in the hive.

          A pangram that uses each letter once is exactly 7 letters long. Players call it a perfect pangram. It scores 14.

          A worked example

          On June 17, 2025, the center letter was M. The other six were A, F, I, N, R, and Y. The Times accepted 30 words worth 125 points.

          • ARMY, FARM, and FIRM have 4 letters, so each scores 1.
          • MARINA scores 6, one point per letter.
          • INFIRMARY uses all seven letters. It scores 9 for its length and 7 for the pangram: 16.
          • FAIRY fits the letters but has no M, so it doesn't count.
          • Genius was 88 points, 70% of 125.

          Before it had seen that day's answers, the solver's likely list for those letters held 29 words, and 27 were answers. MARIA and MIRY were not. ANIMA and MIRIN were answers, but the solver had them in the rarer list, and MARM was not in its list at all. That puzzle is now in the solver's history, so typing MAFINRY shows the Times' 30 words.

          Why the solver's list is not the Times' list

          The Times edits its own word list and doesn't publish it. It leaves out proper nouns, hyphenated words, offensive words, and words it judges too obscure.

          This solver starts from the 178,562-word Scrabble list the board solvers use. 47,250 of those words fit Bee rules: four or more letters, no S, and no more than seven different letters. The solver drops 85 trademarks and offensive words, then splits the rest in two.

          • Likely: 22,327 words that are also in SCOWL size 60, a list of common English words. On 24 Times puzzles from December 2024 to February 2026, this list held 85% of the answers, and 94% of its words were accepted.
          • Rarer: 24,838 words. The Times takes few of them. They sit in a closed list under the answers.

          Each morning the solver also reads the Times' answer list. A word the Times accepts moves up to likely. A likely word it turns down moves to the rarer list. For today's puzzle the solver uses the Times' own list, so nothing is a guess. The about page credits SCOWL.

          Tips for finding the pangram

          • Start with an ending. With I, N, and G in the hive, try words ending in -ING. With E and D, try -ED.
          • Put the center letter in the middle of a word, not only at the start.
          • The pangram hint gives the length and the first letter. Write down the letters your other words haven't used yet.
          • A pangram has at least seven letters, so look at the 7-letter and longer columns of the hint grid.

          Frequently asked questions

          Is this the official NYT Spelling Bee?+

          No. Word Game Helper is not affiliated with The New York Times. The puzzle is on the Times website, and the Times' answer list is the final word.

          What counts as a word in Spelling Bee?+

          Four or more letters, the center letter at least once, and only the seven letters in the hive. Letters can repeat. The Times leaves out proper nouns, hyphenated words, and offensive words.

          How is Spelling Bee scored?+

          A 4-letter word scores 1 point. Longer words score 1 point per letter. A pangram, which uses all seven letters, adds 7 more.

          How many points do you need for Genius?+

          70% of the day's total, rounded. Queen Bee means finding every word. The solver shows both numbers once the letters are in.

          Why does the solver list words the Times rejects?+

          The Times does not publish its word list. For letters you type, the solver guesses from a Scrabble list and from answers the Times has accepted before. Today's puzzle uses the official list, so it has no extras.

          Does Spelling Bee use the letter S?+

          No. The Times leaves S out of every puzzle. If you type an S, the solver asks you to check the letters.

          When does a new Spelling Bee come out?+

          At 3 a.m. Eastern every day. The Today's puzzle button and the answers page switch over soon after.

          Does the solver save my letters?+

          Your letters and found words stay in this browser's storage. The letters you type are not sent anywhere. Clear removes them.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Scrabble bingo bonus?+

Using all 7 tiles in one turn adds 50 points in Scrabble. The same play is a 40-point sweep in Crossplay and a 35-point bingo in Words with Friends.

How many blank tiles are in Scrabble?+

Scrabble has 2 blank tiles. Enter each one as ? on your rack. The rack will not accept a third blank. Crossplay has 3.

Is the Scrabble center square a double word?+

Yes. Scrabble's center star is a double-word score, and the first word must cover it. Crossplay's center star does not multiply the score.

Does this Scrabble word finder use Scrabble letter values?+

Yes. This page opens the solver on Scrabble's letter values. Nine letters differ from Crossplay: B is 3 in Scrabble and 4 in Crossplay; G is 2 in Scrabble and 4 in Crossplay; H is 4 in Scrabble and 3 in Crossplay; J is 8 in Scrabble and 10 in Crossplay; K is 5 in Scrabble and 6 in Crossplay; L is 1 in Scrabble and 2 in Crossplay; U is 1 in Scrabble and 2 in Crossplay; V is 4 in Scrabble and 6 in Crossplay; W is 4 in Scrabble and 5 in Crossplay.

What word list does this Scrabble solver use?+

This helper uses one shared list for Scrabble, Crossplay, and Words with Friends: the older TWL, plus the two-letter words DA, GI, PO, TE, EW, and OK. The official NASPA list is NWL2023. Longer words added since 2006 are not in this list.

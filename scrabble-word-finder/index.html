Scrabble strategy

The center star doubles the opening word, and a seven-tile play then adds 50. That 50 is its own line, after the double. H is 4. G is 2 and U is 1, so those tiles build shape and the high tiles carry the score. J is 8. Each corner is a triple word. On the center row, column 4 and column 12 are double letters. The opening shift puts a high letter on one of those and keeps the star inside the word.

A blank on the star scores 0 and still doubles the rest of the word. The letter chart for every game is the comparison. The order of bonuses is how a score is calculated.

JACKPOT on an empty Scrabble board

With the board empty, enter JACKPOT and press Show Me Best Move. The first lines are Across at 8,4 and Down at 4,8. Both score 110. J is on the double letter in column 4, so the 8 becomes 16. P is on the center star, which doubles the word.

Frequently asked questions

Does the Scrabble center star double JACKPOT?+

The center star is a double-word score, and the opening word has to sit on it. In JACKPOT across at 8,4, P is the tile on the star. The letter total of 30 becomes 60, and the bingo then adds 50, for 110. Down at 4,8 is the same 110.

Why is a centered JACKPOT 94?+

Across at 8,5 puts K on the star and leaves J off the double letter in column 4. The face value is 22, the star doubles it to 44, and the bingo adds 50. Show Me Best Move lists the shifted line, 110, ahead of that 94.

What is JACKPOT worth along the top row?+

Across row 1, columns 1 through 7, J is on the corner triple word and K is on the double letter. The letters total 27, the triple word makes 81, and the bingo adds 50. The main word is 131. That row leaves the star empty, so the play needs a hook already on the board. The hook scores on top of the 131.

How many blanks does this Scrabble rack accept?+

Scrabble's bag has two blanks. This rack accepts two question marks and refuses a third. A blank played as the P on the star in JACKPOT still doubles the other letters. That opening is 104: 27 doubled to 54, then the 50.

How do I enter a Scrabble board on this page?+

Click a square and type the tiles already in play. This page opens with Scrabble letter values, the 50-point bingo, two blanks, and the double-word star. Screenshot reading lives on the Crossplay solver.

Which dictionary does this Scrabble solver check?+

This page checks the solver's 178,562-word file. Club Scrabble now uses NWL2023, which has 17,991 longer words this file lacks. With Scrabble selected, a trademark the Times removed from Crossplay can still be played when the file has it. The word checker shows one spelling.

Where does the 50 sit in the score breakdown?+

The breakdown adds 50 after the main word and any cross-word. The star's double is already inside the main-word figure. JACKPOT's panel is 30 times 2, which is 60, then 50, then 110.

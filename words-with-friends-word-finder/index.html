Frequently asked questions

What is the Words with Friends bingo bonus?+

Using all 7 tiles in one turn adds 35 points in Words with Friends. Scrabble's bingo is 50 points. Crossplay's sweep is 40.

How many blank tiles are in Words with Friends?+

Words with Friends has 2 blank tiles. Enter each one as ? on your rack. Crossplay has 3.

Does Words with Friends have a center star?+

No. The center square has no score bonus, and the board does not show a star. The first word still has to cover the center. Scrabble's center star is a double word. Crossplay shows a star that does not multiply the score.

Does this word finder use Words with Friends letter values?+

Yes. This page opens the solver on Words with Friends letter values, not Scrabble's or Crossplay's. What word list does this Words with Friends solver use?+

This helper uses one shared list for Words with Friends, Scrabble, and Crossplay: the older TWL, plus the two-letter words DA, GI, PO, TE, EW, and OK. Words with Friends has its own official list, which this helper does not load separately.

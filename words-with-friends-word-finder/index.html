Words with Friends strategy

The first word has to cover row 8, column 8, and that square adds the letter's face value. The opening points are on the center row: double letters in columns 7 and 9, double words in columns 4 and 12. The center column is a different lane. It holds triple letters, so a word played down it misses those double words. This layout has 12 triple-word squares, 10 double-word squares, 12 triple-letter squares, and 38 double-letter squares.

The bingo is 35, added after the multipliers. N is 2. B, C, M, and P are 4. Y is 3. With that many double letters and triple words, a short word on a triple word is often the higher line. The full chart is the comparison of the three games.

EXCLAIM on an empty Words with Friends board

Clear the grid, type EXCLAIM, and press Show Me Best Move. The first line is Across at 8,6 for 97. X is on the double letter in column 7, so the 8 becomes 16. L is on the double letter in column 9, so the 2 becomes 4. M, which is 4 here, sits on the double word in column 12. C is on the center square at face value, 4.

QUIZ on a triple word

QUIZ across row 1, columns 4–7, puts Q on a triple word and Z on a triple letter. Z is 10 × 3 = 30 before the word is tripled, so the tripled word turns that 30 into 90. Four tiles means there is no bingo. The line leaves the center empty, so it needs a hook. A hook's points are counted after that 129, which is already higher than the seven-tile EXCLAIM opening.

Frequently asked questions

What does the Words with Friends center square score?+

This board draws no star. The first word still has to cover row 8, column 8, and that square scores the letter's face value. In EXCLAIM across at 8,6, C is the center tile and it scores 4.

Why is the best EXCLAIM played across for 97?+

Show Me Best Move lists Across at 8,6 at 97. X on the double letter in column 7 is 16, L on the double letter in column 9 is 4, and M on the double word in column 12 doubles the 31-point letter total to 62. The bingo adds 35. Starting in column 5, Across at 8,5 scores 61.

Why is EXCLAIM lower down the center column?+

The center column holds triple letters. The double words on an opening sit on the center row, in columns 4 and 12. The best EXCLAIM down the center column is Down at 3,8 for 76, so the across line stays first.

How can QUIZ score 129 with four tiles?+

QUIZ across row 1, columns 4 through 7, puts Q on a triple word and Z on a triple letter. Z is tripled to 30 before the word is tripled, and Z's share of the total is 90. The main word is 129, with no bingo. The play has to hook, and any cross-word is added after the 129. That 129 is higher than the seven-tile EXCLAIM opening.

Why is N worth 2 on this board?+

On this board N scores 2. A hook that adds only N is worth two before any bonus square under the new letter. B, C, M, and P score 4. Y scores 3. The bingo on a seven-tile play is 35, added after those bonuses.

How many blanks does this Words with Friends rack accept?+

The Words with Friends bag has two blanks. The rack keeps two question marks. Two blanks make the search try every letter in both spots. A line that starts several empty squares before a tile already on the board can be left out. Type the word you expected and compare it with the scores that came back.

Does this page use the Words with Friends word list?+

Words with Friends ships its own word list. This page scores a different file. A spelling that exists only on the Words with Friends list will be missing here. The word checker tests one word against the file this solver does load.

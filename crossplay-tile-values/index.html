Vowels and face value

Crossplay has 40 vowel tiles out of 100: 9 A, 12 E, 8 I, 8 O, and 3 U. Scrabble has 42.

Face value is the points on every tile added together. The Crossplay bag totals 210. The Scrabble bag totals 187. The Crossplay total is about 12% higher.

Frequently asked questions

What are the Crossplay tile values?+

With NYT Crossplay selected, Word Game Helper scores letters from the table on this page. That table also lists how many of each tile are in the 100-tile bag. A blank scores 0. Using all 7 tiles adds 40 points.

Which Crossplay letters differ from Scrabble?+

Nine letters differ: B is 4 in Crossplay and 3 in Scrabble; G is 4 in Crossplay and 2 in Scrabble; H is 3 in Crossplay and 4 in Scrabble; J is 10 in Crossplay and 8 in Scrabble; K is 6 in Crossplay and 5 in Scrabble; L is 2 in Crossplay and 1 in Scrabble; U is 2 in Crossplay and 1 in Scrabble; V is 6 in Crossplay and 4 in Scrabble; W is 5 in Crossplay and 4 in Scrabble.

How many tiles are in Crossplay?+

The Crossplay bag has 100 tiles. 97 are letter tiles and 3 are blanks.

How many blanks and S tiles?+

Crossplay has 3 blanks and 5 S tiles. Scrabble has 2 blanks and 4 S tiles.

What are the highest-value tiles?+

J, Q, and Z are worth 10 points. X is worth 8. K and V are worth 6. The bag has one each of J, Q, Z, X, and K, and two V tiles.
